Human-Centered Conversational AI

Takyoung Kim

김탁영 (金卓映)

Hello! I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Conversational AI Lab at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign Computer Science, advised by Prof. Dilek Hakkani-Tür. Previously I received my Master's degree at Korea University, advised by Prof. Pilsung Kang.

My main research interest lies in solving problems related to human-AI communication in real-world. In particular, I am interested in building conversational agents that can seamlessly interact beyond the textual domain, understand social nuances, and provide personalized assistance.

And I love sports! These days I am enjoying running 🏃‍♂️, bouldering 🧗‍♂️, and tennis 🎾.
