# School logo sources

These marks identify the universities listed in Education and belong to their respective universities.

- `uiuc.svg`: [Illinois web brand resources](https://web.brand.illinois.edu/logos/), [original SVG](https://cdn.brand.illinois.edu/logos/block-i.svg).
- `korea.png`: [Korea University symbol guide](https://www.korea.edu/en/1011/subview.do), [original PNG](https://www.korea.edu/sites/en/images/contents/symbol_1.png).
- `skku.png`: [Sungkyunkwan University symbol guide](https://www.skku.edu/skku/about/symbol/symbol_01.do), main color symbol from `BS_01.eps` in the [official AI/EPS download](https://www.skku.edu/_res/skku/etc/ai_1.zip). Rasterized with a transparent background, cropped to the symbol, and resized for the web; the symbol's colors and proportions are preserved.
