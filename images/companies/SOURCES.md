# Company logo sources

These marks identify former employers in Work Experience and belong to their respective companies. Images are stored locally to avoid third-party requests during page visits.

- `meta.svg`: [Meta](https://www.meta.com/) SVG site icon, [original asset](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/rsrc.php/yf/r/-7pQO6hUGK_.svg).
- `amazon.png`: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/) site icon, extracted from the [official favicon](https://www.amazon.com/favicon.ico).
- `lg.png`: [LG AI Research](https://www.lgresearch.ai/) site icon, extracted and resized from the [official favicon](https://www.lgresearch.ai/favicon.ico).
- `naver.png`: [NAVER](https://www.naver.com/) touch icon, [original asset](https://s.pstatic.net/static/www/nFavicon96.png).
